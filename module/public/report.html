Exigency Reporting Form

This form is intended to report any incident, mishap, or concern that requires attention from Co-Founders, HOS, Principals, CMO, Respective Department Heads & Safety Officer.

The Form Responses are routed straight to the concerned department.

Please fill it out within 24 hours of the incident (preferably as soon as possible). For confidential matters, contact the concerned individual directly via email.

Examples of what to report include, but are not limited to:

  • Conflicts: Physical fights within the staff or publically threatening behavior
  • Near Misses: Safety hazards such as slippery floors or loose fixtures
  • Bus/Vans Incidents: Accidents, students left behind, or parents boarding buses/vans
  • Event Mishaps: Injuries during field trips or school events (in school and out school)
  • Internal Mishaps: Kitchen accidents, faulty equipment, or minor injuries
  • Staff Injuries: Falls or incidents occurring during work hours
  • Fire Hazards: Alarms triggered, fire, or related damage
  • Animal Sightings: Snakes, dogs, or other animals on campus
  • Infrastructure Issues: Major electrical, plumbing, or building problems
  • Safety & Security related issue

Mark this issue as Critical if it involves or may lead to immediate danger, injury, or serious risk to student or staff safety, especially in cases involving children.

Critical issues require urgent attention from the leadership team.

Examples:

  • 🔴 Student injury, medical emergency, or visible trauma
  • 🔴 Electrical hazards, fire, or significant infrastructure failure
  • 🔴 Transport accidents, missing child, or serious staff misbehavior in transit
  • 🔴 Foreign body in food that causes or could cause food poisoning
  • 🔴 Physical fights, serious misconduct, or threat to life/safety
  • 🔴 Animal sightings that pose a safety threat (e.g., snake, aggressive stray dog)

Use Not Critical for issues that are concerning but do not pose an immediate risk, such as:

  • 🔵 Minor conflicts or misbehavior
  • 🔵 Foreign body in food discovered before consumption and with no health effects
